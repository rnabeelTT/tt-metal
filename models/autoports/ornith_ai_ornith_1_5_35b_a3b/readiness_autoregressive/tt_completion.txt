 something strange: the sun seemed to rise from the same spot every single morning, and the shadows of the trees shifted in a predictable way.

### **1. What Is Measurement? The Foundation of Understanding**

"Measurement," Grandpa began, "is how we compare something unknown to something known. When you say a tree is 10 meters tall, you're comparing its height to a standard unit — the meter. Without units, numbers are meaningless. A length of '5' tells us nothing. But '5 meters' tells us everything."
