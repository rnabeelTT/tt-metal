# Demo library licenses

This library is the non-audio subset of the EmbeddingGemma 2 demo library (same files, byte-identical; the six-clip compilation video is not included).

Every file in this library comes from a source with a license that permits redistribution. The table lists each item. Query media under `queries/` are not searchable items, but they are redistributed and are listed in the second table.

## Attribution notice

Items marked CC BY or CC BY-SA are used under the Creative Commons Attribution or Attribution-ShareAlike license named in the row. The author column gives the attribution, the source URL gives the original file, and the license URL gives the license text. Images were resized to at most 640 px on the longest side and re-encoded as JPEG. Videos were trimmed, scaled to at most 480 px wide and re-encoded as H.264 without audio. Text items are the first part of the Wikipedia article at the revision given in the source URL. Code items are unmodified excerpts. Derivatives of CC BY-SA works are shared under the same CC BY-SA license as the original. No endorsement by any author is implied.
